## What is FLAC Player Offline: Gapless?

**FLAC Player Offline: Gapless** is a truly offline music player for iPhone that plays the music you already own — MP3, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, OPUS and OGG — in full lossless quality, with no internet connection, no ads, no subscription and no account.

It is built for people who have a real music library: years of purchased tracks, ripped CDs, Bandcamp downloads, hi-res FLAC collections. Instead of renting your own music back through a streaming subscription, you point the app at your files and press play. Everything works offline, forever, and your files never leave your device.

Where most iOS "music player" apps bolt banner ads onto a basic player or lock the equalizer behind a "Pro" tier, this player ships the features audiophiles actually ask for — **real gapless playback**, **crossfade**, a **10-band equalizer** with presets and bass boost, and **ReplayGain** volume levelling — for free, with zero tracking.

## How FLAC Player Works

**1. Add your music.** Pick the folders that hold your files, or beam tracks over Wi-Fi from your computer's browser — no cable, no iTunes sync.

**2. Your library organises itself.** Tracks are sorted by album, artist, genre and folder automatically. Browse however you think about your music.

**3. Press play.** Gapless playback, crossfade, the 10-band EQ and ReplayGain all work offline. Your files never leave the device — no analytics, no tracking, no account.

**Does it need an internet connection?** No. The app works identically in airplane mode. It has no need for a network connection for any core feature.

**Where does my music live?** On your device, in your storage. The app reads your local files; it does not upload them anywhere.

## FAQ

**Is FLAC Player really free?** Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account. It is free to download and use.

**Does it play FLAC and other lossless formats?** Yes — MP3, FLAC, ALAC, WAV, OPUS and OGG, all in full quality. It is ideal for hi-res FLAC libraries.

**What is gapless playback and why does it matter?** Gapless playback removes the tiny silence many players insert between tracks. On live albums, concept records, classical works and DJ mixes, songs flow into each other exactly as intended — no jarring gap.

**How do I get my music onto the phone?** Two easy ways: use **Wi-Fi transfer** to open a page in your computer's browser and drag files straight in, or import folders through the iOS Files app. No cable and no iTunes sync required.

**Does it need an internet connection?** No. Everything runs offline. You can keep the phone in airplane mode and the app works identically. It never needs a connection for playback.

**Does it collect my data?** No. There is no analytics, no tracking and no account. Your music files never leave your device. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

**Can I edit song tags and album artwork?** Yes. The on-device tag editor lets you fix titles, artists, albums and cover art without connecting to a computer.

**Does it support CarPlay?** Yes. Browse and play your local library on the car screen.

**Can I have separate playlists for different situations?** Yes. Multiple play queues let you keep, for example, a workout queue and a commute queue side by side. It also imports and exports M3U playlists.

**Is there a sleep timer?** Yes. Set a timer and the music fades out and stops — perfect for falling asleep to an album.

**What is ReplayGain?** ReplayGain levels the perceived volume across your library so you don't have to reach for the volume control between a quiet track and a loud one.

**Is Android supported?** The app is currently available on the App Store for iPhone. Check the developer's store pages for the latest platform availability.
